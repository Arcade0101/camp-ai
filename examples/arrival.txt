Cedar Creek Science Camp
Arrival guide (fictional test document)

Meet at the visitor center at 8:30 a.m. on the first day.
Check in at the welcome desk and collect your group assignment.
The coordinator briefing begins at 8:45 a.m. in the main lodge.

Bring a refillable water bottle, a notebook, and closed-toe shoes.
If you arrive after the briefing, check in at the visitor center.
